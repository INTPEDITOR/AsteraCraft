AsteraCraft audio credits

Spoken unit voices and faction narrators: generated using ElevenLabs
https://elevenlabs.io

Citrus creature voices and combat / ability / transport sound sources:
generated using VARCO Sound
https://sound.varco.ai

Character direction, short dialogue, sound selection, editing, radio processing,
biomechanical layering and stone telepathy processing: AsteraCraft project.
This is a noncommercial experimental game project.

The source records in data/elevenlabs_voices_v060.json and
data/varco_sounds_v060.json document the generation sources. The active voice
and sound manifests document the edited clips and their source checksums.
Runtime sound cues include edited derivatives of the generated recordings.
These credits do not imply that every cue was generated independently.
